MEME ROBINHOOD CITY

A night drive down the main street Every building belongs to a meme, the statue belongs to the cat

01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Meme Robinhood City

The city where every legendary meme owns a building. You drive down the main street at night, in the rain, past the Doge tower, the Pepe bank, the Troll arcade, until you reach the square with the statue of the cat in the Robin Hood cap. The chain built the city, the memes moved in.

Everything is rendered like a 2003 console game, low-poly, green, with fog and street lamps.

PLACE
One long avenue at night, skyscrapers behind, a full moon, rain
LANDMARK
The green cat statue with the Robin Hood cap and bow at the end of the street
THE FORMAT
Scroll is the drive. The camera moves down the street and the buildings pass by in 3D
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · THE STREETWho lives where

03 · CONTROLSUsing the site

THE DRIVE
Scroll down to drive forward, scroll up to reverse. The camera eases, the buildings grow and pass, the fog thins as they get close
PROGRESS
The bar in the corner shows how far down the street you are
SOUND
Quiet until you tap. Your first click anywhere starts a slow synth loop
THE SQUARE
Past the statue the street ends. Below it the buildings on tiles, then the contract, BUY, X and this page

04 · TOKENToken

The token launches on Robinhood Chain through the Pons launchpad. The contract goes onto the sign at the end of the street and here. Until then both say soon and the copy button says not yet.

CONTRACTsoon
Buy on Pons
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, EVM, addresses start with 0x
LAUNCHPAD
Pons, ponsfamily.com. Buy opens the launchpad, and the token page once the address is in
CHART
DexScreener on Robinhood Chain once the token is live

05 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The city is a handful of pictures on a 3D stage, the drive is your scroll position.

MUSIC
One CC0 synth loop from OpenGameArt, plays after your first tap
REDUCED MOTION
If your system asks for it, the sway, the rain and the neon stop, the scroll still drives

06 · FAQQuestions

Who runs the cityThe cat with the bow, from the square. Nobody voted, nobody complained
Why is it always nightThe memes work nights. The chain never closes either
Can I get out of the carNo. The street is the whole site, the square is the end
What happens at launchThe contract goes onto the sign at the end of the street, the buy button starts pointing at the token page, the lamps stay on
